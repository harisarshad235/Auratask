Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Takt (“we”, “our”, or “us”), hosted at https://takt.harisarshad.site/. Takt is a productivity and task execution workspace created and maintained by Haris Arshad. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your personal information when using our application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information strictly necessary to provide and operate Takt:

  • Authentication & Account Information: When you sign up or log in using Google OAuth 2.0 or email credentials, we receive your basic public profile information:
    • Full Name / Display Name
    • Email Address
    • Profile Picture / Avatar URL
    • Unique authentication identifier (user ID) provided by Supabase / Google
  • User-Generated Content: Tasks, task titles, descriptions, due dates, estimated times of arrival (ETAs), deadlines, priorities, tags, subtasks, notes, comments, and productivity logs that you explicitly create within the application.
  • Local Cache Data: Local storage settings such as theme preferences (dark/light mode), view layout preferences (board vs. list), and temporary offline workspace data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and grant you access to your private workspace.
  • To store, synchronize, and display your tasks, notes, and comments across your devices in real-time.
  • To maintain Row-Level Security (RLS) so that only you can access, edit, or delete your own tasks and records.
  • To diagnose technical errors and improve system stability.
Google User Data Compliance: Takt’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do NOT use Google user data for advertising, marketing, or to train generalized artificial intelligence models.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

We rely on trusted enterprise infrastructure providers strictly for hosting and authentication:

  • Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0): Used for secure single sign-on (SSO).
  • Supabase: Used as our managed backend database and authentication provider. Data is stored with encrypted connections (SSL/TLS) and protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All data transmissions are encrypted in transit via industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. User content in the database is isolated per user account.

We retain your personal data and task records for as long as your account remains active. If you delete specific tasks or comments, they are immediately removed from your active database.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the following rights concerning your data:

  • Access: You can view all your tasks, notes, comments, and profile information inside your workspace at any time.
  • Correction: You can update or edit your tasks and profile information directly in the application.
  • Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Upon receiving your request, all tasks, comments, notes, and authentication records linked to your account will be permanently purged within 7 business days.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke Takt’s access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Security Settings.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Takt does not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We use standard browser local storage (`localStorage`) strictly to store your active authentication session tokens and client preferences (such as dark mode and sidebar state).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. Your continued use of Takt after modifications constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
