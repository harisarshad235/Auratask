This Privacy Policy applies to the Takt web application (“Takt”, “we”, “our”, or “the Service”), accessible at https://takt.harisarshad.site/. Takt is developed, owned, and operated by independent software engineer and organization Haris Arshad.

Takt is a high-velocity personal and team task command center, Kanban cadence workspace, daily planner, and Pomodoro focus manager. This policy provides a comprehensive, transparent explanation of how our application accesses, collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses user information, with specific focus on data obtained via Google Identity Services (Google OAuth 2.0).

1. What Google User Data is Accessed by Our Application

Our application will access the data you have provided to us when you elect to sign in using Google Single Sign-On (SSO). We may collect, or process on behalf of our customers, the following categories of personal data when you use or interact with our products and services:

  • Your Name: Your full name, display name, and given name as provided by your Google account.
  • Your Email Address: The primary email address associated with your Google account.
  • Your Profile Picture / Avatar URL: The URL link to your public Google account profile image.
  • Unique Google Account Identifier (Subject ID): A unique alphanumeric identifier provided by Google (openid / sub claim) to identify your account across sessions.

The specific OAuth 2.0 scopes requested from Google Identity Services are:

OAuth Scope Data Field Received Specific Purpose of Use
openid Unique Google User ID (Subject identifier) To establish a secure, unique account key in our database to isolate your workspace records.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Email Address To identify your account, send critical account-related security notices, and prevent duplicate accounts.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile Display Name & Profile Picture URL To display your name in the workspace header and your avatar on comments and task activities you create.

Zero Sensitive Scopes Guarantee: Our application does NOT request, access, read, or store any sensitive or restricted Google user data such as Google Drive files, Gmail messages, Google Calendar entries, contacts, location data, browsing history, or payment credentials.

2. How Our Application Uses Google User Data

We will use your data to provide you with the services you requested. Specifically, we use your Google user data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. Authentication & Account Management: To verify your identity, establish and maintain your secure login session, and allow you to log in to Takt seamlessly without managing separate passwords.
  2. Database Workspace Isolation: To cryptographically isolate and protect your personal tasks, Kanban boards, and scratchpad notes using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) bound to your unique Google user ID (auth.uid() = user_id).
  3. User Profile Presentation: To personalize your interface by displaying your name in the application header, and showing your avatar beside comments and activity history you post on tasks.
  4. Cross-Device Real-Time Synchronization: To sync your tasks, duration estimates, deadlines, checklists, and notes in real time across multiple computers and web browsers.
  5. Application Stability & Support: To troubleshoot user-reported issues and maintain system performance.

Strict Limitation of Use: We use Google user data ONLY for providing or improving our application’s core functionality. We do NOT use Google user data for reasons other than providing or improving your application’s functionality. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or training generalized artificial intelligence (AI) / machine learning (ML) models.

3. With Whom We Share, Transfer, or Disclose Google User Data

We will not sell your data to third parties, but we may share it with our partners who help us provide our services.

We do not transfer or disclose your information to third parties for purposes other than the ones provided. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your Google user data or personal information to third parties, data brokers, advertising networks, or marketing companies.

We do not transfer Google user data to third parties for reasons other than providing or improving our application’s functionality. We only transfer or disclose data to vetted, essential infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate Takt:

  • Google LLC (Google Identity Services): Used to provide OAuth 2.0 authentication and verify identity tokens. Subject to Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase Inc. (Database & Authentication Host): Managed cloud PostgreSQL database and auth provider where your encrypted account profile, task records, and notes are securely stored. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (CDN & Security Proxy): Provides DDoS mitigation, global edge delivery, and secure TLS/HTTPS termination for web application traffic.

4. Data Protection Mechanisms for Sensitive Data

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data. We use encryption to protect your information and apply industry best practices to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between your web browser, our frontend application, and our backend APIs is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Plaintext HTTP connections are automatically upgraded.
  • Encryption at Rest: All database records, user identifiers, profile records, and task contents are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption via our cloud database provider (Supabase).
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Our database employs PostgreSQL Row-Level Security policies. Every query requires a verified JSON Web Token (JWT) matching the user’s unique Google ID (auth.uid() = user_id). No user can query or modify another user’s data.
  • Defense-in-Depth HTTP Security Headers: We enforce strict HTTP headers including Content Security Policy (CSP), Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy.

5. Disclosures About Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data and Google user data only for as long as your account is active and as necessary to provide the services you requested.

Users have complete ownership and control over their data, including the following deletion options:

  • In-App Immediate Deletion: You can delete any individual task, note, comment, or checklist item at any time using the delete icon inside the Takt interface. Deleted items are immediately removed from our active database.
  • Permanent Account & Complete Data Erasure: You can request permanent, irrevocable deletion of your account and all associated cloud data by emailing the developer at:
    harisengr73@gmail.com with the subject line “Takt Data Deletion Request”.
    Upon receipt, all your profile data, tasks, notes, comments, and authentication tokens will be permanently expunged from all active databases and automated backups within 7 business days.
  • Revoke Google OAuth Access: You can disconnect Takt from your Google account at any time by visiting the Google Account Permissions Center.

7. User-Created Workspace Data & Local Storage

When you use Takt, you may create productivity content including:

  • Tasks & Metadata: Task titles, descriptions, due dates, scheduled times, estimated duration / ETAs, priority flags (P1–P4), tags, subtask checklists, and completion statuses.
  • Activity Logs & Comments: Task comments, status transition records, and Pomodoro session logs.
  • Meeting Notes: Content created inside the Meeting Scratchpad tool.
  • Browser Local Storage: To preserve your UI preferences (dark/light theme, Kanban board vs. list layout) and cached tokens, non-tracking state is stored in your browser’s localStorage.
