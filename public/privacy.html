1. What Google User Data is Accessed by Our Application

When you elect to sign in to Takt using Google Single Sign-On (SSO) or link your Google Account, our application accesses standard identity claims provided by Google Identity Services. We access only non-sensitive identity data strictly necessary to authenticate your account and provision your isolated cloud workspace:

  • Your Name: Your full display name, given name, and family name linked to your Google Account.
  • Your Email Address: The primary verified email address associated with your Google Account.
  • Your Profile Picture / Avatar URL: The public HTTPS image URL pointing to your Google profile picture.
  • Unique Google Subject Identifier (Subject ID / sub claim): A permanent, unique alphanumeric identifier issued by Google to distinguish your account across login sessions.
  • OAuth Token Metadata: Expiration timestamps and authentication token lifecycles issued by Google during OAuth handshake.

The specific OAuth 2.0 scopes requested from Google are limited to the following basic profile scopes:

OAuth 2.0 Scope Data Field Received Specific Feature Dependency Necessity
openid Google Subject ID (sub) Cryptographically binds your workspace records to your unique account key. Required for authentication
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Verified Email Address Identifies your account, prevents duplicate accounts, and receives service notices. Required for account identification
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile Full Name & Profile Photo URL Displays your name in the workspace header and your avatar on comments and task logs. Required for workspace profile presentation

Zero Sensitive or Restricted Scopes Guarantee: Takt does NOT request, access, inspect, or store any sensitive or restricted Google user data such as Google Drive documents, Gmail messages or threads, Google Calendar entries, contacts/address book data, geolocation data, browsing history, or payment/financial credentials.

To provide full task management and focus tracking capabilities, Takt collects and stores the following categories of information that you create or transmit while using the application:

3. How Our Application Uses Google User Data & Information

We use your personal data and Google user data strictly to deliver, maintain, and optimize the features of Takt. Specifically, data is used for:

  1. Authentication & Session Management: To verify your identity via Google OAuth 2.0, establish an authenticated JSON Web Token (JWT) session, and eliminate the need for separate passwords.
  2. Database Row-Level Security (RLS) Enforcement: To cryptographically restrict database queries in our PostgreSQL cloud database so that only your verified Google user ID (auth.uid() = user_id) can read, create, update, or delete your tasks and notes.
  3. Workspace Personalization: To display your name and profile avatar in the navigation header, user settings dropdown, task comments, and activity audit trails.
  4. Cross-Device Real-Time Synchronization: To sync your tasks, checklists, notes, and Pomodoro focus timers across your mobile devices, laptops, and web browsers in real time via Supabase Realtime WebSockets.
  5. Customer Support & Bug Resolution: To troubleshoot user-reported issues and maintain application uptime and security.

Strict Limitation of Use: We use Google user data ONLY for providing or improving our application’s core functionality. We do NOT use Google user data for reasons other than providing or improving your application’s functionality. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or training generalized artificial intelligence (AI) / machine learning (ML) models.

6. Data Protection Mechanisms for Sensitive Data

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data. We use encryption to protect your information and apply industry best practices across all software layers:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser, our web servers, and database APIs is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 over HTTPS). Plaintext HTTP traffic is rejected and automatically upgraded.
  • Encryption at Rest: All database records, user profiles, tasks, and notes are encrypted at rest using industry-standard Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256) through our cloud database host (Supabase).
  • PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS): Every table in our database enforces strict RLS policies. Database operations are cryptographically checked against the authenticated user’s JSON Web Token (JWT). No user can query, modify, or view another user’s records.
  • Defense-in-Depth HTTP Headers: We enforce strict HTTP response headers including Content-Security-Policy, Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: DENY, and Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin-allow-popups.
